# Italy Travel Guide - Part 1: Rome and Florence

## Rome (The Eternal City)
* Overview: Rome is the historic capital city of Italy. The local currency is the Euro (EUR).
* Top Attractions: The ancient Colosseum amphitheater, the Roman Forum ruins, the Pantheon temple, and the Trevi Fountain.
* Vatican City: An independent city-state enclave located inside Rome. It features St. Peter's Basilica and the Vatican Museums, which houses Michelangelo's famous Sistine Chapel ceiling painting.
* Local Culinary: Famous for traditional Roman pasta dishes including Rigatoni alla Carbonara, Cacio e Pepe, and Bucatini all'Amatriciana.

## Florence (The Cradle of the Renaissance)
* Overview: Located in the heart of the Tuscany region, Florence is a global hub for classical art and architecture.
* Top Attractions: The Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore (known as The Duomo) with Brunelleschi's brick dome, the Uffizi Gallery (housing Botticelli's Birth of Venus), and the Accademia Gallery.
* Famous Artwork: The original white marble statue of David by Michelangelo is located inside the Accademia Gallery.
* Shopping & Crafts: Widely recognized for high-quality authentic leather goods and gold jewelry workshops along the medieval Ponte Vecchio bridge.
