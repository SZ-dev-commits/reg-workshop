# Italy Travel Guide - Part 2: Venice, Milan, and Practical Tips

## Venice (The Floating City)
* Transport Infrastructure: Venice is built on more than 100 small islands in a lagoon. There are absolutely no cars allowed. Transportation relies entirely on water buses called Vaporetto or traditional wooden rowing boats known as Gondolas.
* Top Attractions: St. Mark's Square, St. Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, and the winding Grand Canal.

## Milan (The Fashion Capital)
* Overview: A fast-paced northern city recognized as a global capital of fashion, design, and commerce.
* Top Attractions: The enormous Gothic Duomo di Milano, the luxury shopping arcade Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, and the famous mural "The Last Supper" by Leonardo da Vinci at the Santa Maria delle Grazie convent.

## Essential Practical Tips for Travelers
* Best Seasons: Spring (April to June) and Autumn (September to October) offer mild weather. July and August are hot and crowded.
* Coffee Rules: Italians usually drink espresso quickly while standing up at the bar. Ordering a milk-heavy Cappuccino after 11:00 AM is considered a cultural mistake.
* Restaurant Fees: Tipping is not expected. Most restaurant bills automatically include a small per-person cover charge listed as 'Coperto' on the receipt.
* Train Validation: Always validate regional paper train tickets at the green or yellow stamp machines before boarding to avoid expensive on-board fines.
